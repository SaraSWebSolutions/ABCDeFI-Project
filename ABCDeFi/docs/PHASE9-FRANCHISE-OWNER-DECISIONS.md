# Phase 9 — Franchise Owner Decisions

## How to use this decision sheet

The authoritative `ABCDeFI.pdf` does not define a Franchise product or territorial Franchise NFT. FRA-01 through FRA-32 and the supplemental security decisions B-01 through B-04 are explicitly **OWNER-APPROVED**. They are owner policy, not whitepaper-defined rules. Unapproved behavior remains **WHITEPAPER UNSPECIFIED — DO NOT INVENT** and excluded from implementation.

The latest FRA-18/FRA-19 Registry-controlled transfer model is authoritative. B-01 through B-04 supply the approved ownership invariant, lifecycle transitions, request rules, and pause scope. See `PHASE9-FRANCHISE-SECURITY-PRE-AUDIT.md` for the complete security approval record and `PHASE9-FRANCHISE-SPEC.md` for the reconciled canonical specification. Proposal and legacy material cannot override these approvals.

## Supplemental owner-approved security decisions

These decisions resolve the four recorded security blockers. Full rules, permission matrices, and verification criteria are recorded in Sections 4 and 5 of `PHASE9-FRANCHISE-SECURITY-PRE-AUDIT.md`.

| Decision | Owner-approved rule | Related FRA decisions | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B-01 | Registry operator always equals current ERC-721 owner. Establish on mint, update atomically on Registry transfer, check consistency on sensitive operations, and fail closed on mismatch without silent repair. No separate operator-only transfer authority. | FRA-05, FRA-18, FRA-19 | **OWNER-APPROVED** |
| B-02 | Only ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, REVOKED. Only ACTIVE -> SUSPENDED, SUSPENDED -> ACTIVE, ACTIVE -> REVOKED, SUSPENDED -> REVOKED. REVOKED is terminal. Only ACTIVE allows transfer request/approval/execution. Suspension preserves the NFT and history. | FRA-18, FRA-19, FRA-22, FRA-23 | **OWNER-APPROVED** |
| B-03 | Current operator requests transfer naming Franchise/token, current operator, and proposed operator. Authorized administrator approves proposed operator. Execution verifies request validity, recorded current operator versus current owner, Registry operator versus owner, ACTIVE status, and administrative eligibility; consumes the request. Current operator may cancel its own pending request; authorized administrator may cancel/invalidate pending requests. Ownership/operator changes make the request stale and non-replayable. No arbitrary expiry duration or KYC/KYB. | FRA-14, FRA-15, FRA-18, FRA-19, FRA-23 | **OWNER-APPROVED** |
| B-04 | While paused block mint, territory registration, transfer request/approval/execution, suspension, reactivation, and revocation. Reads remain available. Unpause is restricted to the authorized production emergency/admin role; no public or automatic unpause. History is unchanged. | FRA-22, FRA-23, FRA-25, FRA-26 | **OWNER-APPROVED** |

All 32 FRA decisions and all four B decisions are recorded as approved. Economic and integration exclusions remain in force; a later change requires explicit owner approval.

## Explicitly prohibited until approved

- Reusing the legacy nine-tier geography, `$1,000`/`$10,000`/etc. prices, 2–9 bps commissions, three-year lock, 100% ABCD rebate, or 10-way commission model.
- Minting a territorial right, selling one, listing it, accepting payment, distributing revenue, or presenting a KYC status.
- Linking Franchise to Legion, lending, LoanNFTs, referrals, marketplace, Treasury, or Reserve.
- Deploying `FranchiseNFT.sol` as a Phase 9 production implementation.
